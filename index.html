Pending Request Cleaner

Cancel your sent Instagram follow requests in small batches, from your own logged-in browser.

This page never touches Instagram or your account. It only builds a script for you to copy. You run it yourself in Instagram's browser console, so your login stays on your device.

1. Set your batch


  
  




2. Run it


    
  
  1. Log in to Instagram on a desktop browser and open your pending requests list (Settings, then Friends and followers, then Follow requests, then Sent).
    2. 
  
  2. Press F12 (or Ctrl+Shift+J) and open the Console tab.
    3. 
  
  3. If your browser blocks pasting, type allow pasting and press Enter.
    4. 
  
  4. Paste the script and press Enter. Watch the console for progress.
    5. 
  
  5. Wait a few hours, then run another batch.
    6. 




Good to know


    
  
  • Instagram changes its page layout often. If nothing happens, the button text may differ; edit the matching line in the script.
    • 
  
  • Automating actions goes against Instagram's Terms of Use and fast, repeated actions can get an account temporarily limited. Keep batches small.
    • 
  
  • Only paste scripts you have read. A malicious script in the console can take over your account.
